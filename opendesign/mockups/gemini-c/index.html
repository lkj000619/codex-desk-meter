Candidate C: Implementation & Embedded Handoff Notes

Design Intent: Industrial Field Gauge — Rugged monospace aesthetic built for durability, harsh environments, and instant glances. Strong physical compartment framing gives tactile solidity on the 3.16" wide form-factor.

RGB565 Palette Deep Charcoal: #0d0e10 (0x0862), Amber Primary: #f59e0b (0xFD41), Bright Amber: #fbbf24 (0xFDE4), Border Gray: #36393f (0x39E7), Block Off: #23252a (0x2125).

Font Strategy Big Gauge: 54px (38×54 rasterized numbers, 2.8KB total for digits 0-9), Section Headers: 11px bold (8×11 ASCII bitmap), Metadata lines: 9px (6×9 compact font).

Segmented Rendering 15-block progress meters are pre-calculated as 15 small solid rectangles (14×12px), eliminating floating point anti-aliased gauge rendering on the MCU. Direct framebuffer blitting takes under 150 microseconds.
