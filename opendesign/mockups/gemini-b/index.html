Candidate B: Implementation & Embedded Handoff Notes

Design Intent: Swiss Studio Meter — Uncompromising clarity, editorial typography, stark contrasts, and calm readability. Ideal for an architect or designer desk workspace where glare is controlled and calm legibility is paramount.

RGB565 Palette Warm Cream BG: #f5f4ef (0xF7BE), Deep Ink Charcoal: #18181b (0x18C3), Warm Header Bar: #eae8e1 (0xEF7C), Accent Blue: #0284c7 (0x0438), Border Gray: #d4d4d8 (0xD6BA).

Font Strategy Giant Stat: 46px (34×46 raster font, numbers 0-9 + % only: 11 glyphs = 2.2KB), Subheads: 18px-20px (14×20 font, 3KB), Labels & Ledger: 11px-12px (8×12 standard ASCII font ROM).

Rendering & Memory Asymmetric two-column division: Column 1 (430×264) updates primarily on quota tick (once every 1-5 mins); Column 2 (390×264) updates on session tokens. Clean separation minimizes flicker on non-buffered ST7701 lines.

Measurements & Limits Dirty rect 171KB DMA transfer rate and 60fps are unmeasured engineering estimates (`not_run` on hardware). Real ST7701 RGB interface refresh rate and DMA bandwidth must be profiled on ESP32-S3 hardware.
